YOUR MUSIC FOR RESKATE - HOW TO INSTALL
=======================================

You need: skate. on Steam, already working with ReSkate on this PC.

This isn't a normal mod, so it doesn't go in the Mods folder. Normal mods can
only add things like songs, maps and outfits; they can't talk to Spotify or
Apple Music. So this zip updates ReSkate itself, and then builds a small
"Your Music" mod into your Mods folder for you.

1. Close skate. and the ReSkate launcher.

2. Open your skate. folder:
   Steam -> right-click skate. -> Manage -> Browse local files.
   (It's the folder with Skate.exe in it.)

3. Copy EVERYTHING from this zip into that folder (beside Skate.exe,
   NOT into Mods). If Windows asks, choose "Replace the files in the destination".

4. Open the new YourMusic folder and double-click "Install Your Music.bat".
   Wait until it says Done. The first time it downloads ffmpeg (about 100 MB),
   which it needs to build the mod. It puts the finished mod in Mods\YourMusic.

5. Start ReSkateLauncher.exe from your skate. folder.
   If Windows says "Windows protected your PC", click "More info", then "Run anyway".
   Open MODS and make sure YourMusic is turned on. Then press PLAY.

6. A note in the top-left corner should say "ReSkate loaded (development build)".
   That means it's our version.

7. Open Spotify or Apple Music and play a song. Then, in the game, open the music
   screen and pick the "Your Music" playlist. The game goes quiet and your music plays.
   Then go through TEST CHECKLIST.txt.

Run "Install Your Music.bat" again whenever skate. or ReSkate updates.

To go back to normal ReSkate: download it again from
https://github.com/Dingo-Shenanigans/ReSkate/releases and copy it over these files.
