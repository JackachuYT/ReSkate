YOUR MUSIC FOR RESKATE - HOW TO INSTALL
=======================================

You need: skate. on Steam, with ReSkate already working on this PC.

You only do this once. After that, Your Music keeps itself AND ReSkate up to
date automatically, in the background, with no pop-ups.

1. Close skate. and the ReSkate launcher.

2. Open your skate. folder:
   Steam -> right-click skate. -> Manage -> Browse local files.
   (It's the folder with Skate.exe and ReSkateLauncher.exe in it.)

3. Copy ReSkate.dll and the YourMusic folder from this zip into that folder,
   NOT into the Mods folder. When Windows asks, choose "Replace the file in the destination".
   (Your Music lives inside ReSkate.dll, because normal mods can't talk to
   Spotify or Apple Music.)

4. Open the YourMusic folder and double-click "Install Your Music.bat".
   Wait until it says Done. It:
     - builds the "Your Music" playlist mod into your Mods folder (the first time
       it downloads ffmpeg, about 100 MB, which it needs for that),
     - turns off the ReSkate launcher's own updates (Your Music does ReSkate's
       updates from now on, so the launcher doesn't swap its ReSkate.dll back),
     - sets up the automatic updater.

5. Start ReSkateLauncher.exe and press PLAY. A note in the top-left corner should
   say "ReSkate ...-yourmusic... loaded". That means Your Music is running.

6. Open Spotify or Apple Music and play a song. Then, in the game, open the music
   screen and pick the "Your Music" playlist. The game goes quiet and your music plays.

AUTOMATIC UPDATES: every hour, while skate. isn't running, Your Music checks for a
new version (a new ReSkate or a Your Music fix) and installs it. It also rebuilds
the playlist mod after skate. updates. What it did is written to YourMusic\update.log.
It shows up in Windows Task Scheduler as "Your Music for ReSkate updater".

TO UNINSTALL: double-click YourMusic\Uninstall Your Music.bat, start the ReSkate
launcher once (it puts the normal ReSkate.dll back), then delete the YourMusic folder.
